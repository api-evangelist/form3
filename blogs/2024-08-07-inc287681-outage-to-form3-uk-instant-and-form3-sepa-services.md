Aug 7 , 08:00 UTC Resolved - Form3 experienced an outage to the Form3 - UK Instant and Form3 - SEPA services. We have investigated the issue and can confirm during this time both Inbound and Outbound payment processing was impacted. Form3 - UK Instant services were impacted between 07:37 - 07:42 UTC and Form3 - SEPA services between 07:37 - 08:07 UTC.
