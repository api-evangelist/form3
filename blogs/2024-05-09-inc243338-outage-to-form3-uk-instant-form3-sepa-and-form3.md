May 9 , 16:46 UTC Resolved - Between 15:26:00 and 15:27:18 UTC Form3 were alerted to an outage to the Form3 - UK Instant, Form3 - SEPA, and Form3 - Confirmation of Payee services. Payment admission and submission flows were impacted, along with any CoP requests during the impact window. Following a period of monitoring we can confirm BAU service has been restored.
