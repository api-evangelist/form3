Jul 8 , 21:16 UTC Resolved - Between 21:16 and 21:33 UTC, Form3 experienced a service degradation affecting multiple services. During this time, some customers may have experienced increased latency across the impacted services. The issue has now been resolved, and after a period of monitoring we have confirmed there is no ongoing impact.
