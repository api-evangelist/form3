Oct 6 , 20:12 UTC Resolved - Between 18:22–18:30 UTC and 18:33–18:43 UTC, Form3 experienced a service degradation affecting multiple services. During this time, some customers may have experienced increased latency across the impacted services. The issue has now been resolved, and after a period of monitoring we have confirmed there is no ongoing impact.
