Jun 4 , 17:29 UTC Resolved - From 15:07 to 15:26 UTC, Form3 experienced a degradation to the Form3 - UK Instant - Direct (IRE) and Form3 - UK Instant - Direct Non Settling services. During this period, customers may have experienced latency with outbound payment processing. Inbound payments were not impacted.
