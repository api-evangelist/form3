Feb 28 , 14:19 UTC Resolved - From 10:26 to 10:31 UTC, Form3 experienced a degradation to the Form3 - UK Instant (MQ and WS) Service. During this period, there was impact to both inbound and outbound payment flows. The issue was resolved at 10:31 UTC and after a period of monitoring, our Incident Response Team have confirmed there is no ongoing impact.
